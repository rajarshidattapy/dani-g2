# Voice-over script (about 50 seconds)

The captioned video is demo/dani-g2-demo.mp4. These lines match its screens, in order.

1. This is Dani on G2. Dani prepares work for me: emails, calendar changes, file shares. The glasses show what needs my OK. This is sample data, not my real inbox.
2. I open the queue and pick the first draft. I see exactly what Dani wants to send.
3. One click approves. It runs, and I get a receipt.
4. Next, a file share. I approve it. This run fails on purpose in the simulator so you can see the failure path.
5. I retry from the glasses and it goes through.
6. For anything I am unsure about, I reject or skip. Dani drops it and logs it.
7. When the queue is clear, the receipts screen shows what ran, what I skipped and what I rejected.
8. Nothing runs without my click. That is the whole idea: Dani does the work, I keep the decision.
